Connect your school's Moodle

SAIL reads your courses, deadlines, announcements, files and grades, and finds exam and test dates for you. It only reads. It never submits or posts, and everything stays on this computer.

  1. Sign in with your school account

    This opens your school's own sign-in page in a new tab. SAIL never sees your password.

  2. Copy the app link back here

    After you sign in, Moodle shows a page with a link like Click here to launch the app, and your browser may ask to open an app. Cancel that. Then right-click the link, choose Copy link address, and paste it below. It starts with sailmoodle://token=. The sign-in link works for 10 minutes.

Use a security key instead

If your Moodle shows a key for "Moodle mobile web service" under Preferences → Security keys, you can paste it here.
